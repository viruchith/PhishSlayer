# Security policy

PHISH SLAYER is a local proof of concept. Do not expose the API or UI to untrusted networks.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Maintainer contact:** replace this line with an email or security contact of your choosing  
(`SECURITY_CONTACT_TODO@example.com`).

Please include a short description, impact, and steps to reproduce. Do not include real employee data or production secrets in reports.

## Scope notes

- The PoC has **no authentication**. Treat it as a localhost demo.  
- Known intentional limitations (mock MCP, in-memory telemetry, keyword policy) are documented in [docs/THREAT_MODEL.md](./docs/THREAT_MODEL.md).
